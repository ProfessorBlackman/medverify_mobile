# MedVerify — Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 (Build 2) — Beta

Thanks for testing MedVerify! Here's what's new in this build.

### New Features

- **See other possible matches, not just the best one.** When a scan or search turns up more than one candidate product, you'll now see a "Other Possible Matches" section below the top result — ranked by confidence, with product name, manufacturer, and registration/expiry dates at a glance. Tap any of them to view the full details.
- **Revamped How It Works screen.** A clearer walkthrough of the three ways to verify a product — Search, Scan Barcode, and Multi-Evidence Verify — with updated step-by-step guidance.
- **Expanded Info Hub.** Now includes a full Medicine Safety Guide, FAQs, quick actions, and direct links to FDA Ghana resources and support.

### Improvements

- **Verification result cards now match the product's actual status.** Colors, icons, and badges on a product's card (verified, expired, recalled, unregistered, etc.) are now consistent with that product's real registration status, instead of a generic neutral style.
- **Privacy Policy and Terms of Service redesigned.** Both are now organized into clear, expandable sections that are much easier to scan, with updated and more complete content on data collection, your rights, and how information is used.
- **About screen refreshed.** Clearer description of what MedVerify does, an official data source card, database info, and easier ways to reach support or view legal info.
- **More reliable app startup.** Fixed an issue where the app could get stuck on the loading screen for an extended time on a slow or unreliable connection — it now always opens promptly, and retries verifying your device automatically the next time you use a feature that needs the network.

### Fixes

- **Fixed incorrect status badges in Scan History.** Some verified, non-expired products were incorrectly showing up labeled as expired or unapproved in your scan history and on the dashboard's recent scans — this is now corrected.
- Minor text and contact-detail corrections across the About screen.
