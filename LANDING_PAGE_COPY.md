# MedVerify

**Know what's in your hands before you take it.**

MedVerify is a mobile app that helps you check whether a medicine is registered with the Ghana Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) — in seconds, right from your phone.

Unregistered medicines are a real problem across Ghana, and nothing on a pack tells you whether the product behind it was ever approved. MedVerify gives everyday people a fast, simple way to check a medicine's registration status with the FDA before they buy or take it.

---

## How It Works

1. **Scan or search.** Point your camera at a medicine's barcode or packaging, or manually enter its registration number or name.
2. **We cross-check the evidence.** MedVerify compares what you scanned — barcode, registration number, manufacturer, active ingredient, and more — against official FDA Ghana registration records.
3. **Get a clear result.** You'll see the product's registration status, confidence level, and full details (manufacturer, expiry date, registration date, and more) in one place.
4. **Help others.** If you want, you can optionally contribute the price you paid and where you bought it, helping build a community picture of pricing and of where unregistered products are turning up.

No single piece of evidence has to be perfect — MedVerify weighs everything you provide together, so a smudged barcode or a partially visible label doesn't mean you're stuck.

---

## Why It Matters

- **Peace of mind in seconds.** No more guessing whether a medicine is legitimate — get an answer before you buy or take it.
- **Works with whatever you can scan.** Barcode, packaging photo, or just the registration number — MedVerify uses multiple signals together, not just one.
- **Backed by official data.** Results are checked against FDA Ghana's own registration records, not a third-party guess.
- **Built for everyone.** Simple enough for a first-time user, useful enough for pharmacists, caregivers, and health workers.
- **A community effort.** Every optional contribution — a price, a pharmacy location, a report — helps other users spot patterns and stay safer.
